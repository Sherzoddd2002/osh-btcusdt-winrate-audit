Derivatives data availability: order-book history and liquidation stream history cannot be reconstructed from today's public REST snapshot; those feeds must be captured prospectively. fundingRate supports historical queries with startTime/endTime pagination. In contrast, openInterestHist exposes only the latest month, and global/top-trader Long/Short ratio endpoints expose only the latest 30 days. Therefore, OI and ratio data cannot support a 2020–2026 historical backtest; start collecting them now and evaluate them only on a later, separately held-out period. Har juma GO/NO-GO ni qayta ko'rib chiqish

Bu eng xavfli band. 90 kunlik fold 1 soatlik gorizontda mustaqil kuzatuvni har 90 kunda bir marta beradi. Haftalik "qayta ko'rib chiqish" amalda haftalik qayta sozlashga aylanadi: holdout buziladi, ko'p sinov tarafkashligi yig'iladi, albatta "omadli" konfiguratsiya topiladi.

Men shunday qildim: juma avtomatizatsiyasi faqat ma'lumot yaxlitligi + drift + mavjud mezonlar holatini kuzatadi, parametr/feature/threshold'ni o'zgartirmaydi, holdout'ni ochmaydi. GO/NO-GO qarori faqat yangi to'liq mustaqil fold to'planganda — chorakda bir marta.

4 ta strategiyani "alohida sinash"

To'g'ri, lekin 4 strategiya × 5 fold × bir necha parametr = o'nlab sinov bitta ma'lumot to'plamida. Oldindan ro'yxatga olinmasa, eng yaxshisi tasodifan chiqadi. Deflated Sharpe / Bonferroni, har strategiya uchun bitta oldindan yozilgan gipoteza, va bitta umumiy sinov byudjeti shart.

Yana: short tomonni shunchaki long'ning oynasi deb bo'lmaydi — funding, borrow, likvidatsiya assimmetriyasi va BTC'ning tarixiy musbat drifti bor. Alohida xarajat modeli kerak.

7. "Foydali qilish"ning halol tartibi

  1. O'lchovni tuzatish — qilindi (v3). Bunsiz foyda bilan omadni ajratib bo'lmaydi.
  2. O'yinni o'zgartirish: gorizontni 1h dan 4h/1d ga ko'chirish + savdolar sonini kamaytirish. Bu model emas, arifmetika: xarajat/harakat ulushi 26% → 12.8% → 4.8%.
  3. Orqaga yuklanadigan pul oqimi (funding, OI, taker ratio, basis) — bitta oldindan yozilgan gipoteza, bitta ablatsiya.
  4. Meta-labeling: oddiy qoida nomzod chiqaradi, ML faqat "xarajatdan keyin foydalimi?" deb filtrlaydi; default = NO TRADE.
  5. Eshik: median net > 0, musbat fold ≥ 75%, PF > 1, bootstrap CI pastki chegarasi > 0, benchmarkdan ustun, look-ahead = 0. O'tmasa — 6–8 hafta shadow, keyin juda kichik kapital.
